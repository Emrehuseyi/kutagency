03 / QUESTIONS ANSWERED

BEFORE
YOU VISIT.

What should I consider before choosing PPF?

Consider intended use, the areas needing coverage, desired finish and the installation process. This is demo content; verify actual products and scope with the business.

Does coating replace physical film protection?

These are different protection approaches. Film provides a physical layer; coatings can improve surface characteristics. Ask a qualified installer what makes sense for your vehicle.

Can I book directly through this concept?

No. This is an independent, uncommissioned creative study. A commercial launch would require the studio's approval and a live booking integration.
